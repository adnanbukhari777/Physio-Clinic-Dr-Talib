Privacy & Preferences

Cookie & Third-Party Services Policy

This static demonstration website does not use tracking, advertising, marketing, or analytics cookies. It does not collect patient records or track visitor activity across websites.

Third-Party Services: Google Maps

To help visitors locate Physio Clinic in Al Fareed Garden, Pakpattan, this website includes an interactive Google Map. To protect your privacy, this map is blocked by default and will not load until you choose to load it.

When you click “Load Interactive Map” or “Load Map”, your browser makes a direct connection to Google’s servers, which may log your IP address and standard browser request headers in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.

If you prefer not to load the embedded map, you can use the direct Get Directions link at any time to open the clinic location directly in your chosen navigation app without enabling embedded third-party scripts.

Local Storage Preference

When you grant or decline permission to load the interactive map, your preference is stored locally in your browser using the key physio_clinic_map_consent_v1 (with a value of granted or declined). This preference is stored solely on your device so the website remembers your choice on subsequent visits.

Reset Your Map Preference

You can clear your stored map preference at any time by selecting the button below. Once cleared, the map will return to its blocked state, and the preference prompt will reappear when you visit the homepage.
